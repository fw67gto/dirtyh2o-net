History

Dirty H2O aka “Dirty Water” came in to existence as a joke 20 years ago between great friends. The term was what we would say to each other as a catch phrase when referring to situations, people, or experiences that were not going to be long term or were not exactly what we wanted at the moment. The premise being that if you were stranded and wandering in the desert and you were really really thirsty, as much as you would like a nice clean crisp cool drink of water, if you came across “Dirty Water” you would drink it. Over time, the analogy took on its own life and grew from the original inference. And what we realized, and people that we told the phrase to agreed with, was that people are more often than not in situations they don’t necessarily want to be in, but they learn how to get by and move past that situation. The situation could involve a person, job, living situation, relationship, or anything else you may encounter in life. The challenge is being honest with yourself and others regarding the situation you are in, and the situation you currently want, and how to make the most of it. For example, maybe I am in a situation where a Long Term (LT) relationship isn’t ideal at the moment. Typically it is hard to let someone know, you just want to date casually (C.D.) , or maybe only one time (O.T.O.) because you are not from the area, or possibly there is a reason you want to keep it private and not public (N.I.P.). Over the last 20 years we have learned that people want to be honest about the situation they are in, and there is comfort meeting people who understand and can relate to being in that situation. We also identified that there isn’t a forum for people to communicate what they are looking for honestly....and so we created Dirty H2O! Dirty H2O will be the site that allows people to seek and find others that are interested in the same situation. But more than that we will be a forum for people to connect, share, and have fun experiences with other like minded people and businesses. We want our subscribers to be able to make the connection from the Virtual to Reality! Stay Tuned!!
